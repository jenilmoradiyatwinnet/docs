## How credits work

Twinalyze uses credits as a shared usage balance inside each organization.
Credits are added based on the organization’s active plan, deducted as
supported activity is processed, and carried forward when they are not used.

Credits and usage are organization-specific. Credits available in one
organization cannot be transferred to or used by another organization.

## Country-tier pricing

Analytics pricing is based on the country associated with the user who
generated the event. Each supported country is assigned to a pricing tier,
and that tier determines the applicable event rate.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion
    title="Do Twinalyze credits expire?"
    icon="infinity"
  >
    No. Unused Twinalyze credits remain in your account balance and do not
    expire.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="How many credits are included with each plan?"
    icon="coins"
  >
    Free includes 25 credits every month. Growth includes 50 credits every
    month. Enterprise credit allocations are customized according to the
    organization’s requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="Can I purchase additional credits?"
    icon="cart-shopping"
  >
    Additional credit purchases are available with the Growth plan.
    Enterprise credit arrangements are configured according to the
    organization’s agreement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="How does country-tier pricing work?"
    icon="globe"
  >
    Analytics events are priced according to the pricing tier assigned to the
    user’s country. Open the Twinalyze panel to view the latest country-to-tier
    assignments and current rates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="What happens when my Growth credit balance reaches zero?"
    icon="shield"
  >
    Twinalyze activates up to 20 temporary Grace Credits. This continuity
    buffer helps supported services continue without an immediate interruption.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="Are Grace Credits free?"
    icon="circle-exclamation"
  >
    No. Grace Credits are a temporary advance. The amount you use is
    automatically deducted from the next credits added through a purchase or
    plan renewal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="Are all 20 Grace Credits recovered?"
    icon="circle-check"
  >
    Only the Grace Credits that were actually used are recovered. Unused Grace
    Credits are not deducted from the next credits added to your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="How are used Grace Credits recovered?"
    icon="rotate"
  >
    When new credits are added, Twinalyze first deducts the Grace Credits that
    were previously used. The remaining credits become your available balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="How is the Growth plan billed?"
    icon="calendar"
  >
    Growth is displayed as \$18 per month when billed annually. The annual
    billing option includes a 20% saving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion
    title="When should I choose Enterprise?"
    icon="building-shield"
  >
    Choose Enterprise when your organization requires custom usage limits,
    advanced security and governance, enterprise customization, dedicated
    support, or tailored commercial terms.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
