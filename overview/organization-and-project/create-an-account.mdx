<Steps titleSize="h3">
  <Step title="Go to Twinalyze.com">

      ![Twinalyze website home page](/images/create-an-account/Afinal.png)

  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign up">
    Select **Sign up**, enter your email address and password, and continue to create your account.

    <Frame>
      ![Create a Twinalyze account](/images/create-an-account/sign-up.png)
    </Frame>

    
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Enter your organization details and click **Create Organization**.Your organization is used to manage projects, team members, roles, permissions, and settings.

    <Frame>
      ![Create a Twinalyze organization](/images/create-an-account/create-your-organization-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Welcome to dashboard">
    After creating your organization, Twinalyze automatically opens your dashboard.You can now by start by creating project.

    <Frame>
      ![Twinalyze dashboard](/images/create-an-account/welcome-to-dashboard-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit organization

Go to **Settings → Manage Profile → Organization Profile**.

Update your organization name, mobile number, website, or logo, and click **Save**.

<Frame>
  ![Edit organization details in Twinalyze](/images/create-an-account/edit-organization-2.png)
</Frame>

---

## Delete organization

To delete your organization, send a request from your registered email address to [support@twinalyze.com](mailto:support@twinalyze.com).

<Warning>
  Organization deletion permanently removes related projects, users, settings, and analytics data. Back up important information before submitting your request.
</Warning>

## Best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use a valid business email">
    Use an email address that your team can access. A business email is recommended when creating an account for a company.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Create a strong password">
    Use a secure password and do not share your account credentials with other team members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use a clear organization name">
    Use your company, brand, or product name so team members can identify the organization easily.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Invite each team member separately">
    Invite each team member and assign the appropriate role and permissions instead of sharing one account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep organization details updated">
    Update your organization name, contact information, website, and logo whenever your business information changes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
