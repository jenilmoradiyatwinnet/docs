  ## Role-based access and product foundations

  Twinalyze expanded its initial platform with role-based authentication and
  the first supporting product modules.

  ### Role-based authentication

  - Introduced a role-based access-control system
  - Added support for five different role types
  - Allowed access to be managed according to the assigned role
  - Created the foundation for future organization and team permissions
  - Kept administrative capabilities limited during the initial platform stage

  ### Additional product foundations

  - Added the initial Custom Store Listing module
  - Introduced notification capabilities
  - Prepared the platform for project-specific features and controlled user access

  These additions helped Twinalyze move from an initial project structure
  toward a multi-feature analytics platform.
